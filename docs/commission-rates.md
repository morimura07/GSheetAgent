## Payment rules

- **9-month advance:** American Amicable, Americo, Mutual of Omaha, Corebridge (all products) and Transamerica. Advance = 9 months of year-1 commission.
- **6-month advance:** CICA when the client pays by bank draft.
- **3-month advance, at most $1,000:** Generational Life. The advance is paid back first out
  of the commission earned; the rest is paid as earned. "Advance on issue: No": paid when the
  first premium comes in, not when the policy is issued.
- **Medicare:** a flat amount a month per policy (Settings > Plans, "Flat $ a month"),
  whatever the premium. About $36; the client's contract comes in the next weeks, then they
  update the amount (or add one plan per Medicare carrier) in Settings.
- **Everything else:** paid as the premium comes in.
- **Chargeback:** the part of the advance not yet earned (paid 2 of 9 months → owes 7).
- **Death in year 1:**
  - American Amicable charges back only unearned premium on level and graded, but **all** premium on ROP.
  - Aetna and Royal Neighbors charge back 100% if the policy is rescinded.
