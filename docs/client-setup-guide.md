# Setting up your tracker

About 10 minutes. A computer is easier for steps 1 to 4. We can do it together on a call.

## 1. Before you start

- Sign in to the Google account you'll use for the tracker.
- Make sure 2-step verification is on (Google Account > Security > 2-Step Verification).

## 2. Make your copy

1. Open the link I sent you.
2. Click **Make a copy**. You can rename it, for example "Agent Tracker".

The copy is yours: I can't see it or open it.

## 3. Set it up

1. In your copy, wait a few seconds until the **Agent Tracker** menu appears at the top
   (after Help).
2. Click **Agent Tracker > Set up workbook**.
3. Google asks you to allow the tracker:
   1. Click **OK** or **Continue**, then choose your account.
   2. You'll see "Google hasn't verified this app". That is expected for a private tool.
      Click **Advanced**, then **Go to Agent Tracker (unsafe)**.
   3. If you see boxes to tick, tick **all** of them. Then click **Allow**.
4. Click **Agent Tracker > Set up workbook** again. After about a minute the tabs appear
   (Dashboard, Clients, Policies…) and it says "Workbook is ready".

## 4. Make the phone link

1. Click **Extensions > Apps Script**. A new tab opens.
2. Click the blue **Deploy** button (top right), then **New deployment**.
3. Click the gear next to "Select type" and choose **Web app**.
4. Fill in the form:
   - **Description:** Phone app
   - **Execute as:** Me
   - **Who has access:** Only myself
5. Click **Deploy**. If Google asks you to allow it again, do as in step 3.
6. Click **Done** and close that tab. Don't change anything else there.

## 5. Put it on your phone

1. Back in the sheet, click **Agent Tracker > Phone app link**.
2. Get the link to your phone, for example by emailing it to yourself.
3. On your phone, open the link in **Chrome**. Chrome must be signed in to the same Google
   account.
4. Tap **⋮**, then **Add to Home screen**.

## Later: giving your assistant access

1. Click **Agent Tracker > Assistant workbook**, then **Open the assistant workbook**.
2. Click **Share** and add your assistant's email as **Editor**.

They'll see names, phones, lead follow-ups, carrier, policy number and status only. To
stop their access, remove them under Share.

## If something needs fixing

Add me to your tracker for that day (Share > my email > Editor), and remove me when I'm
done.
